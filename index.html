About this data & its limitations

The dataset covers the records returned by the official public directory, not every travel business in India.

Source spelling, spacing, legacy city labels, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses are retained. Contacts have not been called or emailed. Unusual phone formats are flagged with the dataset's original warning, not corrected; an unusual format may be an international number.

The state / union territory filter uses the dataset's state labels, not a verified office location. It can be combined with search, directory city, and classification filters.

CSV exports include English and Arabic names, source identifiers, state labels, classifications, and contact warnings. The JSON download includes the original records, pagination metadata, counts, and scope. Spreadsheet formula-like text is prefixed with an apostrophe in CSV exports for safe opening.

This independent resource is not affiliated with Nusuk or a booking service. Always consult the official Nusuk Masar directory for current information.
